# ISO Clause Reference: Close Paraphrases & Audit Prompts

Source: Tarren Reitsema (ex-NOPSEMA regulator). Authoritative domain input for AXIS audit prompt generation.

Covers: ISO 9001:2015 · ISO 14001:2026 · ISO 45001:2018 + A1:2024

---

## ISO 9001:2015 — Quality Management System

### 1 Scope

**Close paraphrase:** Specifies requirements for a QMS where an organisation needs to show it can consistently provide conforming products/services and enhance customer satisfaction.

**Audit prompts**
- What products/services are covered by the QMS?
- How does the organisation demonstrate consistent conformity?
- How does the QMS support customer satisfaction?

---

### 2 Normative references

**Close paraphrase:** ISO 9000 is the normative reference for fundamentals and vocabulary.

**Audit prompts**
- Are terms used in the QMS consistent with ISO 9000 meanings?
- Are internal definitions aligned or at least not conflicting?

---

### 3 Terms and definitions

**Close paraphrase:** Applies the relevant ISO 9000 terms and definitions.

**Audit prompts**
- Are key QMS terms understood consistently?
- Are internal procedures using terms such as "nonconformity", "documented information", "external provider" correctly?

---

### 4 Context of the organisation

#### 4.1 Understanding the organisation and its context

**Close paraphrase:** Determine internal and external issues relevant to purpose, strategic direction, and ability to achieve QMS intended results.

**Audit prompts**
- What internal and external issues have been determined?
- How do those issues affect quality outcomes?
- How often are they reviewed?
- Show where context issues flow into risk, objectives, process controls, or management review.
- Not a clause or requirement, but worth considering if the organisation itself has deemed climate change is relevant to QMS outcomes.

#### 4.2 Understanding the needs and expectations of interested parties

**Close paraphrase:** Determine relevant interested parties and their relevant requirements because of their effect, or potential effect, on conforming products/services and customer satisfaction.

**Audit prompts**
- Who are the relevant interested parties?
- What requirements have been identified for each?
- Which requirements affect product/service conformity?
- How are changes in these requirements tracked?

#### 4.3 Determining the scope of the QMS

**Close paraphrase:** Define QMS boundaries and applicability, considering context, interested-party requirements, and products/services; justify any non-applicable requirements.

**Audit prompts**
- What is the QMS scope?
- What sites, functions, products, and services are included?
- Are any ISO 9001 requirements excluded or treated as not applicable?
- Is the justification valid and not weakening product/service conformity?

#### 4.4 Quality management system and its processes

**Close paraphrase:** Establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve the QMS, including needed processes and their interactions. Determine process inputs, outputs, sequence, criteria, resources, responsibilities, risks/opportunities, evaluation methods, improvements, and documented information.

**Audit prompts**
- Show the process map.
- What are the inputs and outputs for each key process?
- How do processes interact?
- What criteria are used to control process performance?
- Who owns each process?
- What risks and opportunities are linked to each process?
- What records prove processes are operating as planned?

---

### 5 Leadership

#### 5.1.1 Leadership and commitment — General

**Close paraphrase:** Top management must demonstrate leadership and accountability for QMS effectiveness, policy/objectives alignment, integration into business processes, resources, communication, performance, improvement, and support for relevant roles.

**Audit prompts**
- How does top management demonstrate accountability?
- What QMS issues reach executive/board level?
- How are quality objectives embedded in business planning?
- What resources has leadership approved for QMS effectiveness?
- How does leadership respond to poor quality performance?

#### 5.1.2 Customer focus

**Close paraphrase:** Top management must ensure customer and applicable statutory/regulatory requirements are determined, understood, met, risks/opportunities affecting conformity and satisfaction are addressed, and customer satisfaction is maintained.

**Audit prompts**
- How are customer requirements identified?
- How are legal/regulatory requirements captured?
- How are risks to customer satisfaction identified and controlled?
- What customer satisfaction data is reviewed by leadership?

#### 5.2.1 Establishing the quality policy

**Close paraphrase:** Establish a quality policy appropriate to purpose/context, supporting strategic direction, providing a framework for objectives, and committing to requirements and continual improvement.

**Audit prompts**
- Is the policy appropriate to the organisation's actual work?
- Does it support strategic direction?
- Does it provide a basis for objectives?
- Does it commit to meeting requirements and improving the QMS?

#### 5.2.2 Communicating the quality policy

**Close paraphrase:** Maintain the policy as documented information, communicate it, ensure it is understood/applied, and make it available to relevant interested parties.

**Audit prompts**
- Where is the policy documented?
- How is it communicated?
- Can workers explain what it means for their role?
- Is it available externally where appropriate?

#### 5.3 Organisational roles, responsibilities and authorities

**Close paraphrase:** Assign, communicate, and understand responsibilities/authorities for QMS conformity, process performance, reporting, customer focus, and integrity during change.

**Audit prompts**
- Who is accountable for QMS conformity?
- Who reports QMS performance?
- Who ensures customer focus is maintained?
- How are responsibilities preserved during organisational change?

### 6 Planning

#### 6.1 Actions to address risks and opportunities

**Close paraphrase:** Determine QMS risks/opportunities based on context and interested-party requirements, then plan actions to assure intended results, enhance desirable effects, prevent/reduce undesired effects, and improve. Actions must be integrated into QMS processes and proportionate to product/service conformity impact.

**Audit prompts**
- What QMS risks and opportunities have been determined?
- How do they link to context and interested parties?
- What actions have been planned?
- Are actions proportionate to potential quality impact?
- How is effectiveness of actions evaluated?

#### 6.2 Quality objectives and planning to achieve them

**Close paraphrase:** Establish measurable quality objectives at relevant functions, levels, and processes; align them with policy, requirements, conformity, and customer satisfaction; monitor, communicate, update, and plan how to achieve them.

**Audit prompts**
- What quality objectives exist?
- Are they measurable?
- Which processes/functions own them?
- What resources, responsibilities, timing, and evaluation methods are defined?
- Are objectives updated when performance or context changes?

#### 6.3 Planning of changes

**Close paraphrase:** Changes to the QMS must be planned, considering purpose, consequences, system integrity, resources, and responsibilities/authorities.

**Audit prompts**
- What recent QMS changes occurred?
- Was impact assessed before implementation?
- Were resources and responsibilities updated?
- Was QMS integrity maintained?

### 7 Support

#### 7.1.1 Resources — General

**Close paraphrase:** Determine and provide resources needed to establish, implement, maintain, and improve the QMS, considering internal capabilities and external provider needs.

**Audit prompts**
- What resources are required for QMS effectiveness?
- What gaps exist?
- How are external resource dependencies managed?

#### 7.1.2 People

**Close paraphrase:** Provide people needed for effective QMS operation and process control.

**Audit prompts**
- Are staffing levels adequate for controlled operations?
- Are key roles covered during absence/turnover?

#### 7.1.3 Infrastructure

**Close paraphrase:** Determine, provide, and maintain infrastructure needed for process operation and conformity.

**Audit prompts**
- What infrastructure is needed?
- How is it maintained?
- Are failures affecting quality?

#### 7.1.4 Environment for operation of processes

**Close paraphrase:** Determine, provide, and maintain the process environment needed for conformity.

**Audit prompts**
- What physical, social, psychological, or environmental conditions affect process quality?
- How are they controlled?

#### 7.1.5 Monitoring and measuring resources

**Close paraphrase:** Determine and provide suitable monitoring/measuring resources; ensure they remain fit for purpose; calibrate/verify where traceability is required; protect against invalid results.

**Audit prompts**
- What measuring equipment/resources are used?
- Are they calibrated or verified?
- What happens if equipment is found out of tolerance?
- Are records retained?

#### 7.1.6 Organisational knowledge

**Close paraphrase:** Determine knowledge needed for operations and conformity; maintain it and make it available; address changing knowledge needs.

**Audit prompts**
- What critical knowledge is required?
- Where is it captured?
- How is knowledge protected from loss?
- How are lessons learned incorporated?

#### 7.2 Competence

**Close paraphrase:** Determine competence requirements, ensure competence through education/training/experience, take action to acquire competence where needed, and retain evidence.

**Audit prompts**
- What competencies are required for each role?
- How is competence verified, not just training attended?
- What gaps were identified and closed?
- What records prove competence?

#### 7.3 Awareness

**Close paraphrase:** Ensure relevant people understand the policy, objectives, their contribution to QMS effectiveness, improvement benefits, and consequences of nonconformity.

**Audit prompts**
- Can workers explain the quality policy and objectives?
- Do they understand how their work affects conformity?
- Do they know consequences of nonconforming work?

#### 7.4 Communication

**Close paraphrase:** Determine internal/external QMS communications: what, when, with whom, how, and who communicates.

**Audit prompts**
- What QMS matters are communicated internally?
- What is communicated externally?
- Who is authorised to communicate with customers/regulators?
- How is communication effectiveness checked?

#### 7.5.1 Documented information — General

**Close paraphrase:** Include documented information required by ISO 9001 and that needed by the organisation for QMS effectiveness.

**Audit prompts**
- What documented information is mandatory?
- What additional documented information has the organisation determined is necessary?

#### 7.5.2 Creating and updating

**Close paraphrase:** Ensure documented information has appropriate identification, format/media, review, and approval.

**Audit prompts**
- Are documents identifiable and current?
- Are they reviewed/approved before issue?
- Are formats controlled?

#### 7.5.3 Control of documented information

**Close paraphrase:** Control availability, suitability, protection, distribution, access, retrieval, use, storage, preservation, change control, retention, disposition, and external documents.

**Audit prompts**
- Are current procedures available at point of use?
- How are obsolete documents prevented from use?
- How are records protected from alteration?
- How are external documents controlled?

### 8 Operation

#### 8.1 Operational planning and control

**Close paraphrase:** Plan, implement, and control processes needed to provide products/services and implement Clause 6 actions; determine requirements, criteria, resources, controls, documented information, and change control.

**Audit prompts**
- How are operational processes planned?
- What acceptance criteria apply?
- What records show processes operated as planned?
- How are planned and unintended changes controlled?

#### 8.2.1 Customer communication

**Close paraphrase:** Manage communication about product/service information, enquiries, contracts/orders, feedback/complaints, customer property, and contingency actions where relevant.

**Audit prompts**
- How do customers receive product/service information?
- How are enquiries and complaints handled?
- How is customer property communicated and protected?

#### 8.2.2 Determining requirements for products and services

**Close paraphrase:** Determine customer, statutory/regulatory, organisation-defined, and necessary requirements before offering products/services.

**Audit prompts**
- How are product/service requirements determined?
- How are regulatory requirements captured?
- What requirements has the organisation added itself?

#### 8.2.3 Review of requirements for products and services

**Close paraphrase:** Review requirements before commitment, including customer-specified, delivery/post-delivery, unspecified but necessary, statutory/regulatory, organisation-defined, and contract changes; resolve differences and retain documented evidence.

**Audit prompts**
- Show a contract/order review.
- Were all requirements confirmed before acceptance?
- Were unclear or changed requirements resolved?
- Are records retained?

#### 8.2.4 Changes to requirements for products and services

**Close paraphrase:** Ensure changed requirements are amended in relevant documents and communicated to relevant people.

**Audit prompts**
- Show a changed customer requirement.
- Were affected documents updated?
- Were relevant staff informed?

#### 8.3.1 Design and development — General

**Close paraphrase:** Establish, implement, and maintain a design/development process suitable to ensure subsequent product/service provision.

**Audit prompts**
- Is design/development applicable?
- What design process is used?
- Does it ensure outputs can be delivered?

#### 8.3.2 Design and development planning

**Close paraphrase:** Plan design stages, controls, responsibilities, interfaces, resources, involvement, verification/validation, complexity, and documented information.

**Audit prompts**
- Show a design plan.
- What stages, reviews, verification, and validation are defined?
- Who approves each stage?

#### 8.3.3 Design and development inputs

**Close paraphrase:** Determine functional/performance requirements, prior design information, statutory/regulatory requirements, standards/codes, consequences of failure, and resolve conflicting inputs.

**Audit prompts**
- What were the design inputs?
- Were legal/regulatory requirements included?
- Were conflicts resolved?

#### 8.3.4 Design and development controls

**Close paraphrase:** Apply controls to ensure results are defined, reviews are performed, verification and validation occur, problems are addressed, and records are retained.

**Audit prompts**
- What reviews occurred?
- How was verification performed?
- How was validation performed?
- What design issues were found and resolved?

#### 8.3.5 Design and development outputs

**Close paraphrase:** Ensure outputs meet inputs, are adequate for subsequent processes, include monitoring/measurement and acceptance criteria, and specify essential characteristics for intended use.

**Audit prompts**
- Do outputs trace to inputs?
- Are acceptance criteria defined?
- Are critical characteristics specified?

#### 8.3.6 Design and development changes

**Close paraphrase:** Identify, review, control, and retain information on design changes to prevent adverse effects on conformity.

**Audit prompts**
- Show a design change.
- Was impact reviewed?
- Was approval recorded?
- Were downstream documents updated?

#### 8.4.1 Control of externally provided processes, products and services — General

**Close paraphrase:** Ensure externally provided processes/products/services conform; determine controls for external providers and outsourced processes.

**Audit prompts**
- What externally provided items/processes affect conformity?
- How are providers controlled?
- Are outsourced processes included in the QMS?

#### 8.4.2 Type and extent of control

**Close paraphrase:** Apply controls based on provider impact, effectiveness of provider controls, verification activities, and risk to conformity.

**Audit prompts**
- How is supplier risk assessed?
- What controls are applied?
- How is supplier performance monitored?

#### 8.4.3 Information for external providers

**Close paraphrase:** Communicate requirements for products/services, processes, approval, competence, interactions, performance control, verification, and release.

**Audit prompts**
- What requirements are sent to suppliers?
- Are acceptance and verification requirements clear?
- Are competence or approval requirements specified?

#### 8.5.1 Control of production and service provision

**Close paraphrase:** Provide production/service under controlled conditions, including documented information, resources, monitoring/measurement, infrastructure, competent people, validation of special processes, mistake prevention, release/delivery/post-delivery controls.

**Audit prompts**
- Are work instructions available?
- Are acceptance criteria known?
- Are personnel competent?
- Are special processes validated?
- What error-proofing exists?

#### 8.5.2 Identification and traceability

**Close paraphrase:** Identify outputs where needed and maintain traceability where required.

**Audit prompts**
- How are outputs identified?
- Where is traceability required?
- Can you trace from input to final output?

#### 8.5.3 Property belonging to customers or external providers

**Close paraphrase:** Safeguard customer/provider property while under organisational control; identify, verify, protect, and report loss/damage/unsuitability.

**Audit prompts**
- What customer/provider property is held?
- How is it protected?
- What happens if it is lost or damaged?

#### 8.5.4 Preservation

**Close paraphrase:** Preserve outputs during production/service provision to maintain conformity.

**Audit prompts**
- How are products/materials protected from damage, deterioration, contamination, or loss?
- Are storage and handling controls adequate?

#### 8.5.5 Post-delivery activities

**Close paraphrase:** Meet post-delivery requirements considering legal/customer requirements, consequences, product/service nature/lifetime, customer feedback, and warranty/support obligations.

**Audit prompts**
- What post-delivery obligations exist?
- How are warranty/support/maintenance commitments controlled?
- How is customer feedback used?

#### 8.5.6 Control of changes

**Close paraphrase:** Review and control changes in production/service provision to maintain conformity; retain information on changes, authorisation, and actions.

**Audit prompts**
- Show an operational change.
- Was it reviewed and approved?
- Were impacts on conformity assessed?

#### 8.6 Release of products and services

**Close paraphrase:** Verify requirements are met before release unless authorised otherwise; retain evidence of conformity and release authority.

**Audit prompts**
- What release criteria apply?
- Who authorises release?
- Is evidence retained?

#### 8.7 Control of nonconforming outputs

**Close paraphrase:** Identify and control nonconforming outputs to prevent unintended use/delivery; take appropriate action; verify corrections; retain evidence.

**Audit prompts**
- How are nonconforming outputs identified?
- How are they segregated or controlled?
- Who approves concessions?
- Are corrected outputs reverified?

### 9 Performance evaluation

#### 9.1.1 General

**Close paraphrase:** Determine what needs monitoring/measurement, methods, timing, analysis/evaluation, and retain evidence of results.

**Audit prompts**
- What QMS performance data is monitored?
- Are methods valid?
- How are results analysed?
- What decisions are made from the data?

#### 9.1.2 Customer satisfaction

**Close paraphrase:** Monitor customer perceptions of whether needs and expectations have been fulfilled; determine methods for obtaining/reviewing data.

**Audit prompts**
- How is customer satisfaction measured?
- What trends are visible?
- How are complaints analysed?

#### 9.1.3 Analysis and evaluation

**Close paraphrase:** Analyse and evaluate data from monitoring/measurement to determine conformity, satisfaction, QMS performance, planning effectiveness, risk/opportunity action effectiveness, supplier performance, and improvement needs.

**Audit prompts**
- What data is analysed?
- How is supplier performance evaluated?
- How do analysis results drive improvement?

#### 9.2.1 Internal audit — General

**Close paraphrase:** Conduct internal audits at planned intervals to determine whether the QMS conforms to organisational and ISO 9001 requirements and is effectively implemented/maintained.

**Audit prompts**
- Does the audit programme cover all QMS requirements?
- Are audits testing implementation and effectiveness?
- Are findings linked to evidence?

#### 9.2.2 Internal audit programme

**Close paraphrase:** Plan, establish, implement, and maintain audit programmes considering importance, changes, and previous results; define criteria/scope; select objective auditors; report results; correct issues; retain evidence.

**Audit prompts**
- Is audit frequency risk-based?
- Are auditors independent of audited work?
- Are corrective actions tracked to closure?
- Are audit records retained?

#### 9.3.1 Management review — General

**Close paraphrase:** Top management reviews the QMS at planned intervals to ensure suitability, adequacy, effectiveness, and alignment with strategic direction.

**Audit prompts**
- When was the last management review?
- Did top management attend?
- Was strategic alignment considered?

#### 9.3.2 Management review inputs

**Close paraphrase:** Review status of previous actions, changes in context/interested parties, performance trends, objectives, nonconformities, monitoring results, audit results, customer satisfaction, supplier performance, resources, risk/opportunity action effectiveness, and improvement opportunities.

**Audit prompts**
- Were all required inputs covered?
- Were trend data and risks reviewed?
- Were previous actions closed?

#### 9.3.3 Management review outputs

**Close paraphrase:** Produce decisions/actions on improvement, QMS changes, and resource needs; retain records.

**Audit prompts**
- What decisions resulted?
- Were actions assigned and tracked?
- Were resource needs addressed?

### 10 Improvement

#### 10.1 General

**Close paraphrase:** Determine and select improvement opportunities and implement actions to meet customer requirements and enhance satisfaction.

**Audit prompts**
- What improvement opportunities were identified?
- How were priorities selected?
- What changed as a result?

#### 10.2 Nonconformity and corrective action

**Close paraphrase:** React to nonconformities, control/correct them, address consequences, evaluate need for action to eliminate causes, implement actions, review effectiveness, update risks/opportunities, and change QMS if needed; retain evidence.

**Audit prompts**
- Show a nonconformity from detection to closure.
- Was root cause determined?
- Were similar issues checked?
- Was corrective action effective?

#### 10.3 Continual improvement

**Close paraphrase:** Continually improve QMS suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness using analysis, evaluation, and management review outputs.

**Audit prompts**
- What evidence shows the QMS is improving?
- Are improvements systemic or just local fixes?
- Are trends improving over time?

## ISO 14001:2026 — Environmental Management System

> Note: This content is based on ISO 14001:2026 (Fourth edition). It is not intended to represent ISO 14001:2015 requirements.

### 1 Scope

**Close paraphrase:** Specifies EMS requirements to enhance environmental performance, meet compliance obligations, and achieve environmental objectives.

**Audit prompts**
- What EMS outcomes are intended?
- How does the EMS improve environmental performance?
- How are compliance obligations and objectives managed?

---

### 2 Normative references

**Close paraphrase:** No normative references are required for application.

**Audit prompts**
- Not usually auditable beyond confirming no external normative dependency is being misapplied.

---

### 3 Terms and definitions

**Close paraphrase:** Defines EMS concepts such as environmental aspect, impact, compliance obligation, lifecycle, risk/opportunity, performance, and documented information.

**Audit prompts**
- Are EMS terms used consistently?
- Do aspect/impact/risk/compliance obligation have clear internal meanings?

---

### 4 Context of the organisation

#### 4.1 Understanding the organisation and its context

**Close paraphrase:** Determine external and internal issues relevant to organisational purpose and affecting ability to achieve intended EMS outcomes, including environmental conditions that can affect or be affected by the organisation.

**Audit prompts**
- What internal/external issues have been determined?
- What environmental conditions are relevant?
- How do environmental conditions affect the organisation?
- How does the organisation affect those conditions?
- How are issues reviewed?

#### 4.2 Understanding needs and expectations of interested parties

**Close paraphrase:** Determine relevant interested parties, their relevant needs/expectations, and which become compliance obligations.

**Audit prompts**
- Who are the relevant interested parties?
- What needs and expectations have been identified?
- Which are compliance obligations?
- How were compliance obligations selected?
- How is this kept current?

#### 4.3 Determining the scope of the EMS

**Close paraphrase:** Define EMS boundaries/applicability considering context, interested-party obligations, organisational units/functions, physical boundaries, activities/products/services, authority/control/influence, and lifecycle perspective; maintain scope as documented information and make it available.

**Audit prompts**
- What is the EMS scope?
- What sites/functions/activities/products/services are covered?
- How was control/influence considered?
- How was lifecycle perspective considered?
- Is the scope available to interested parties?

#### 4.4 Environmental management system

**Close paraphrase:** Establish, implement, maintain, and continually improve the EMS, including needed processes and interactions, to achieve intended outcomes and improve environmental performance.

**Audit prompts**
- What EMS processes exist?
- How do they interact?
- How are EMS requirements embedded in business processes?
- What evidence shows the EMS is maintained and improved?

---

### 5 Leadership

#### 5.1 Leadership and commitment

**Close paraphrase:** Top management must demonstrate leadership and accountability for EMS effectiveness, environmental policy/objectives, integration into business processes, resources, communication, intended outcomes, continual improvement, and support for relevant roles.

**Audit prompts**
- How does leadership take accountability for environmental performance?
- Where are EMS issues discussed by top management?
- How are EMS requirements embedded in business planning?
- What resources have been provided?
- How does leadership promote continual improvement?

#### 5.2 Environmental policy

**Close paraphrase:** Establish, implement, and maintain an environmental policy appropriate to purpose/context, including nature/scale/impacts; provide framework for objectives; commit to environmental protection, compliance obligations, and continual improvement. Maintain, communicate, and make available.

**Audit prompts**
- Is the policy appropriate to the organisation's environmental aspects?
- Does it include environmental protection, compliance, and improvement commitments?
- Does it support environmental objectives?
- Can workers explain what it means?
- Is it available to interested parties?

#### 5.3 Roles, responsibilities and authorities

**Close paraphrase:** Assign and communicate responsibilities/authorities for EMS conformity and reporting environmental performance to top management.

**Audit prompts**
- Who is responsible for EMS conformity?
- Who reports environmental performance?
- Are responsibilities understood at operational level?
- Are authorities sufficient to control environmental risks?

---

### 6 Planning

#### 6.1.1 Actions to address risks and opportunities — General

**Close paraphrase:** Establish planning processes considering context, interested parties, EMS scope, aspects, compliance obligations, and risks/opportunities to assure EMS outcomes, prevent/reduce undesired effects, and achieve continual improvement.

**Audit prompts**
- What planning process addresses EMS risks/opportunities?
- How are 4.1, 4.2, and scope inputs used?
- How does planning prevent undesired environmental outcomes?
- How does it support continual improvement?

#### 6.1.2 Environmental aspects

**Close paraphrase:** Determine environmental aspects of activities/products/services within the EMS scope that the organisation can control or influence, considering lifecycle perspective, changes, abnormal conditions, and emergencies. Determine significant aspects using established criteria and maintain documented information.

**Audit prompts**
- What aspects have been identified?
- How were lifecycle stages considered?
- Are normal, abnormal, and emergency conditions covered?
- Were potential emergency situations with environmental impact determined here and fed into 8.2?
- What significance criteria are used?
- Which aspects are significant and why?
- Are aspect records maintained and current?

#### 6.1.3 Compliance obligations

**Close paraphrase:** Determine and access compliance obligations related to environmental aspects, determine how they apply, and take them into account in the EMS; maintain documented information.

**Audit prompts**
- What legal and other requirements apply?
- How are requirements accessed and updated?
- How is applicability determined?
- How are obligations linked to aspects and controls?
- Is the compliance register current?

#### 6.1.4 Risks and opportunities

**Close paraphrase:** Determine risks and opportunities related to environmental aspects, compliance obligations, and other context/interested-party issues that need to be addressed to achieve EMS outcomes.

**Audit prompts**
- What risks and opportunities have been determined?
- How do they link to aspects, compliance obligations, context, and interested parties?
- Which require action and why?
- How are they prioritised?

#### 6.1.5 Planning action

**Close paraphrase:** Plan actions to address significant aspects, compliance obligations, and priority risks/opportunities; integrate actions into EMS or other business processes; evaluate effectiveness.

**Audit prompts**
- What actions address significant aspects?
- What actions address compliance obligations?
- What actions address risks/opportunities?
- Where are actions embedded — objectives, competence, controls, emergency response, monitoring?
- How is effectiveness evaluated?

#### 6.2.1 Environmental objectives

**Close paraphrase:** Establish environmental objectives at relevant functions/levels, considering significant aspects, compliance obligations, risks/opportunities, and policy commitments; objectives should be measurable where practicable, monitored, communicated, and updated.

**Audit prompts**
- What environmental objectives exist?
- How do they link to significant aspects and obligations?
- Are they measurable or otherwise evaluable?
- Who owns them?
- How are they communicated and updated?

#### 6.2.2 Planning actions to achieve environmental objectives

**Close paraphrase:** For objectives, determine actions, resources, responsibilities, timelines, evaluation methods, and how results will be integrated into business processes.

**Audit prompts**
- What actions support each objective?
- What resources are allocated?
- Who is responsible?
- What deadlines apply?
- How are results evaluated?

#### 6.3 Planning of changes

**Close paraphrase:** Changes affecting the EMS must be planned and controlled to maintain EMS integrity.

**Audit prompts**
- What EMS-relevant changes occurred?
- Were environmental impacts and obligations considered before change?
- Were documents, roles, controls, and monitoring updated?
- Was EMS integrity maintained?
